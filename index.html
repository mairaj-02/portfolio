Background

PHASE 01:

I started as a Physics undergrad but I switched to Electrical Engineering cuz of my interest in electricity, magnets, and waves.

PHASE 02:

In EE I developed a strong interest in power engineering. In my third year I realized how essential the field of power electronics and control is for modern grids and I became obsessed with it.

PHASE 03:

While I was taking power electronics course, I tried designing my very own DC-DC power converter which got me into designing PCBs as a side hobby.

PHASE 04:

When designing PCBs, I continuously encountered things like microcontrollers, sensors, communications protocols, and more which naturally built my embedded systems skills. Since microcontrollers need to be programmed, I also started learning programming.

AS OF NOW:

I recently finished my internship at Hong Kong Electric, where I was exposed to Data Analysis and Machine Learning. So now I am trying to hone my skills further in this field.
