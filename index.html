Some Fun Facts and Gentle Intro

I started as a Physics undergrad. I liked electromagnetism, so I switched to EE. When I joined EE, I really liked power engineering, but a professor pointed me towards where power is actually heading, which pulled me towards power electronics and its control topologies — modern power systems lean more and more on renewables. Getting into power electronics got me designing PCBs, which pulled me into embedded systems and how useful they are across different fields. Since that needed programming and software knowledge, I started learning to code with online resources, and I still learn and code for fun on the side. Right now, after my internship at Hong Kong Electric, I'm learning more about data science and AI.

Outside of studies, I play squash for PolyU at the university level, and have somehow kept my GPA above 3.7 through all of it. I was also awarded the Hong Kong Talent Development Scholarship.

I'm a big YouTube nerd too — I love bingewatching techy videos, and some of my favourites are Phil's Lab, ElectrArc 240, Veritasium, ThePrimeagen, and Fern.
